A message to everyone who has spent time with us

Dear friends,

Over the years, so many of you have spent time and given your custom to Woodland Rise, and we have never once taken that for granted. Whether you visited once or returned again and again, you are part of the story of this place.

However, after a great deal of thought, we have made the difficult decision to close until further notice. It is not a decision we have come to lightly.

Thank you once again for your support.

With kind regards,

Trevor and family

Woodland Rise is currently closed until further notice.
